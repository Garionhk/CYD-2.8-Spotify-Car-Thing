CYD Spotify Remote

A Spotify now-playing screen and remote for the 2.8" ESP32 "Cheap Yellow Display" (ESP32-2432S028R). Version 1.0.0.

Install

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer (Web Serial; not Safari, Firefox or a phone).
  2. Plug the CYD into the computer with a USB data cable.
  3. Click Install, pick the serial port (often USB Serial or CH340), and wait about 2 minutes.
This browser can't flash devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Open this page over https (or localhost) to install.
Installing erases the board. Wi-Fi, the Spotify login and the touch calibration are set up again afterwards (a few minutes, below).

After installing

  1. Touch calibration starts by itself: press and hold each target, then tap the centre.
  2. The screen shows a QR code for the hotspot CYD-Spotify-XXXX. Join it with your phone; the setup page opens.
  3. Add your Wi-Fi (and your phone's hotspot for the car — 2.4 GHz, WPA2), then Save.
  4. Back on your home Wi-Fi, open http://cyd-spotify.local and follow the Spotify steps there.

The full guide — creating the Spotify app, the car, troubleshooting — is in the README.

Screen blank or garbled after installing?

Boards sold as ESP32-2432S028R come with one of two display chips. Hold a finger on the screen from power-on for 8 seconds (the blue LED blinks from 4 s): it switches to the other chip and restarts.
